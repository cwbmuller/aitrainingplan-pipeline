## Coach's eye (advisory, openai)

**tweak** (4/5). This is a sensible taper week: volume is cut substantially, there is only one hard session and several full rest days, and the plan preserves neuromuscular sharpness with race‑pace openers and an outdoor hill outing. Positive elements include high‑cadence easy spins on the trainer, an outdoor ride to rehearse climbing position, and limiting hard efforts to avoid residual fatigue. My main concern is that Wednesday's 4 x 3 min threshold efforts and Saturday's 50 min tempo on rolling hills are borderline intensive for a taper this close to the event and could blunt freshness. Tightening the intensity and shortening the length of those key sessions, and swapping the threshold reps for very short sharpeners, will keep you race‑ready without risking fatigue.

Top fix: Replace Wed 4x3min threshold with 6–8 x 30–60s all‑out/near‑race‑pace sharpeners with full recoveries, and shorten Sat to 60–75 min with only 3–4 short (1–2 min) climb efforts at race pace; prioritize easy spins and full rest the final 48 hrs before the event.
