## Coach's eye (advisory, openai)

**tweak** (4/5). This is a well-structured, conservative week that respects Thandi’s constraints: two non-consecutive hard sessions, flat routes to protect the left Achilles, a single long run on Sunday, and total weekly volume sitting just under her recent peaks. Warm-ups, cool-downs, and a full rest day are included — all appropriate given the Achilles niggle and morning-before-work routine. Two small concerns: the 5 x 3-minute 10k intervals are a meaningful tendon load and could aggravate the Achilles if intensity or mechanics slip, and there’s no dedicated strength/calf eccentric work to proactively address the niggle. Also, the week is close to her allowed weekly max minutes, so any progression needs to be cautious.

Top fix: Replace 5x3' intervals with 6x2' at 10k effort (same stimulus, slightly less tendon strain) and add two 12–15 minute strength + calf-eccentric sessions (easy core/hip/slow eccentric calf raises) on Mon and Sat (post-run or evening) to improve Achilles resilience.
